Reading features

What you can do
with CrossRSVP

01

Read full pages

Open books from your SD card, turn pages, and choose your font and text size. Your library and the familiar CrossPoint reading tools are still here.

02

Keep the words in one line

In RSVP, words from your book appear one at a time around a fixed letter. The reader refreshes the line area with the word. In the tested X3 run, that made the word changes faster.

03

Set your reading speed

Choose how many words to show per minute. Pause whenever you need, step forward, or go back to reread. Adjust the pauses for long words and punctuation.

04

Return to the page

Switch from RSVP back to the book to reread a passage or see an image or table. Your reading position is saved when you leave the book.

05

Keep short words together

Enable word grouping to show up to two short supporting words beside the main word. They appear in a smaller font. Leave grouping off to read one word at a time.

Getting started

How to install
CrossRSVP

Already using CrossPoint Reader or CrossRSVP? Update directly from your SD card.

  1. Download your firmware

    Choose your reader above and download its .bin file. Back up the books and settings on your SD card, and keep the previous firmware file.

  2. Copy it to the SD card

    Put the .bin file on the card. If you copied it over USB on X4 Pro, safely eject the drive on your computer before leaving USB Transfer on the reader.

  3. Start the update

    Open Settings → System → SD Card Firmware Update. Select the file, confirm the update, and wait for the restart without turning the reader off.

  4. Open a book and try RSVP

    Open an EPUB and bring up the reading menu. Choose More → RSVP Reading Mode in the toolbar, or RSVP Reading Mode in the list menu. Reading starts paused.

Still using the factory firmware? Start with the CrossPoint installation guide ↗, then follow the steps above.
