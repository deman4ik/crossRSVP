The reader you know,

01

Keep your place

RSVP shares Reading Position with Paged Mode. Pause, return to the page, and pick up where you left off.

02

Find the focus

A fixed ORP keeps one letter in the same place while words change around it. The point is steadiness, not speed promises.

03

Choose the pace

Start paused, adjust WPM, and continue from the current word. RSVP is designed for EPUB; unsupported content returns you to pages.
